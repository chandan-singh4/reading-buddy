What I kept, and what I left behind

Kept: inhale and exhale

The best thing in Argon's design. It says why the two halves belong in one app, which none of my five doors did. Reading takes words in. Journaling lets them out.

Kept: the living lip

The folded half stays on screen as a 54 px strip. This fixes the worry I had about my own door 3 — that the front door would become the only way in. You are never shut inside one mode.

Kept: the bridge

The pill at the seam carries your last highlight into a new entry. It is the book‑linked journaling we already designed, pulled onto the front door. It is also the one element that makes the halves read as one app rather than two.

Kept: tap straight through

The cover goes to page 142. The microphone starts recording. No menu in between. Turn on the second switch to see both marked.

Changed: the clothes

Argon dressed this in Instrument Serif, Inter and amber on slate. Your app wears Playfair Display, EB Garamond and a warm brown that turns to gold at night. Same ideas, your skin. The dark theme here is the app's real dark theme, not a second design.

Dropped: Tailwind, and the emoji

Your theme.css says no hard‑coded colours or sizes in components, so the themes stay one change. Tailwind's utility classes work the other way, and a CDN script would also weigh on an offline app. The emoji labels were swapped for the geometric glyphs your chrome already uses.

My one disagreement: the clock

Argon tilts the split by time of day. I would tilt it by what you did last instead. Your own habit is better evidence than the hour, and a wrong guess from the clock is annoying in a way that a wrong guess from your own behaviour is not. Try both switches and see whether you agree.
